Storm Damage Repair — Common Questions

Does homeowner's insurance cover storm roof damage in California?

Yes — California HO-3 policies typically cover wind, hail, and storm damage. Flood damage requires separate flood insurance. We provide written damage assessments and photo documentation to support your insurance claim.

How quickly should I act after storm damage to my roof?

Immediately — water intrusion compounds quickly. California's prompt notice requirement means you should file with your insurer within a reasonable time after the storm. We can tarp and document damage same day to protect your claim.

Do you work directly with insurance adjusters?

Yes. We've worked with State Farm, Allstate, AAA, Mercury, and Farmers adjusters in Merced County. We can be present during the adjuster's inspection and provide a detailed repair estimate that aligns with Xactimate pricing.
