Cost of Roof Replacement in Merced, CA (2026 Pricing Guide)

Merced Roofing Pros·May 12, 2026·6 min read

A Merced roof replacement runs $8,500–$22,000 for most homes depending on material and size. Here's the 2026 price breakdown and what actually drives cost.

Based on jobs we've completed across Merced County, expect to pay $8,500–$22,000 for a full residential roof replacement in 2026. Here's how that breaks down.

Cost Factors Specific to Merced

  • Heat-rated materials: California Title 24 and CRRC require cool-roof-certified products on most residential replacements. These cost 10–15% more than standard shingles but qualify for utility rebates.
  • Permit fees: Merced City charges a flat $185–$350 for residential re-roof permits depending on valuation. County unincorporated areas run similar. We include this in our quote.
  • Decking replacement: Homes built 1960–1990 often have 3/8" OSB that has delaminated. Budget $2–$4 per sq ft for decking replacement if needed.
  • Attic ventilation: California requires balanced intake/exhaust. Adding ridge vents or soffit vents runs $500–$1,200 depending on existing conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are Merced roof quotes higher than national averages?

California labor rates, strict Title 24 energy compliance, and CRRC-certified material requirements push installed costs 20–30% above national medians. Merced is less expensive than the Bay Area or LA but tracks with Central Valley averages.

Does homeowner's insurance cover roof replacement in Merced?

Storm damage (hail, wind) is typically covered after your deductible. Wear-and-tear replacement is not. We provide detailed damage documentation to support your insurance claim.
