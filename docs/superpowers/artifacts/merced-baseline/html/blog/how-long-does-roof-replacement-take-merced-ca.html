How Long Does a Roof Replacement Take in Merced, CA?

Merced Roofing Pros·May 11, 2026·5 min read

Most Merced homeowners are back under a finished roof in one to three days. Here's exactly what determines that timeline and how to plan around it.

Most Merced roof replacements finish in one to three days. A standard 2,000 sq ft single-story home with asphalt shingles: two days. Larger two-story homes or complex rooflines may push to three.

What Drives the Timeline

  • Roof size: A 25-square (2,500 sq ft) roof takes roughly 8–10 crew-hours for tear-off and another 8 to install. Two-man crew = two days; four-man crew = one day.
  • Pitch: Low-slope (2/12 to 4/12) roofs common on Central Valley ranch homes move faster than steep 8/12 or 10/12 pitches found on older Victorians in downtown Merced.
  • Layers: California allows two layers of asphalt shingles. If your home already has two, tear-off adds a half day.
  • Decking condition: Rotted OSB or plywood sheeting gets replaced on the fly — budget an extra half day for older Merced homes built pre-1990.
  • Material lead time: Asphalt shingles ship from our supplier in Fresno overnight. Metal panels (standing seam) need 2–3 weeks fabrication time — plan accordingly.

Merced's Summer Heat Window

We schedule most replacements before 10 AM and after 4 PM June through September. Shingles seal best between 70°F and 90°F; in Merced's 105°F July heat the adhesive strips can over-soften, so we adjust nailing patterns and seal tabs manually. This adds about 2–3 hours to a summer job — factor that in if you're scheduling during peak heat.

Permit Timeline

Merced City and Merced County both require a roofing permit for full replacements. Merced City Building and Safety typically issues permits within 24–48 hours for residential re-roofs. We pull and pay for permits on your behalf — it's included in our estimate.

What the Schedule Looks Like

  1. Day 1 morning: Dumpster delivery, materials staged, tear-off begins
  2. Day 1 afternoon: Underlayment and ice-and-water shield installed
  3. Day 2: Shingles, flashing, ridge cap, gutters re-hung
  4. Day 2 afternoon: Cleanup, magnetic sweep, final walkthrough

You're usually sleeping under the finished roof on Night 2.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to leave my house during a roof replacement in Merced?

No. You can stay home — the crew works outside. Expect noise from 7 AM to 6 PM. Some homeowners prefer to be away during tear-off (loudest phase), but it's not required.

What happens if it rains mid-job?

We monitor weather forecasts and reschedule around rain. If an unexpected shower hits mid-job, we cover exposed decking with tarps. Merced averages only 13 inches of rain per year, so delays are rare May–October.

How long does a metal roof replacement take vs. asphalt?

Metal panel installation takes 1–2 days longer than asphalt due to the precision cutting and seaming required. Total project time is typically 3–4 days for a standard ranch home.
